Block the apps that steal your time. No hidden charges. Cancel anytime. Nothing leaves your iPhone.

Halto uses Apple's Screen Time APIs to shield the apps you choose, on a schedule, after a daily limit, or during a focus session you start. It has no account, no server and no analytics. It will never tell you a block is impossible to bypass, because on iOS that would not be true.
