FAQ

Good questions

Can't I just turn it off?

Yes, you can. iOS always lets you turn off Halto's blocking permission in Settings or delete the app, and Halto doesn't hide that. What it adds is strict mode (Pro): ordinary unlocks are switched off and one emergency unlock a day is left. While strict mode is blocking, that profile can't be edited, switched off or deleted, and once the emergency unlock is used, the block stands until the schedule ends.

How do I open an app when I really need it?

Tap “I need it” on the shield and Halto sends a notification. Tap it to open the pause: breathe for 15 seconds, pick a reason, and the app opens for 5 minutes. You get three unlocks a day by default; the pause, the unlock length and the daily count can be changed in Settings › Unlock defaults.

Can Halto see which apps I use?

No. Apple gives Halto anonymous handles for the apps you pick, never their names, and your usage numbers are drawn only inside Apple's private sandbox. Halto has no account and no server, so nothing is sent anywhere.

What's free?

One profile (schedule or focus), three focus sessions a day, today's report with your unlock reasons, the weekly summary notification, the Live Activity and the widget. Pro adds unlimited profiles, daily limits, strict mode and insights history beyond today.

Do blocks start and end exactly on time?

Not to the second. iOS wakes Halto when the device is in use, so a block or an unlock can be a little late. The shield comes back when an unlock ends, though it can take a little longer if your iPhone is asleep.

How do I cancel?

In Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, or from Manage subscription inside the app, at any time. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends and you won't be charged. If you like, Halto can remind you once, 24 hours before the trial ends.
